# rama-packaging-voice-agent
This repo consists of an audio recording of an AI Voice Agent made for Rama Packaging. It handles order taking from the caller and saves that to an excel sheet or whatever the CRM the client is maintaining. It can take order, cancel, and engage with any client queries.

This audio is a conversation between a potential lead and the AI Voice Agent. The female voice is the AI that is taking order from the caller. It can be used like this and other purposes include: appointment booking for clinics, salons, etc; queries answering; mass outreaching in peak times; etc.
